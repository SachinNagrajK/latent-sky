Astronomy archives contain extraordinary images, but exploring the evidence behind them usually requires specialist tools. Latent Sky makes that process approachable: choose a real sky field, reconstruct its image, inspect measured sources, and trace every result to its original data. Our live Supabase prototype creates a galaxy mosaic and measures twenty sources near the Coma cluster, with nineteen catalog matches. Visitors can explore and save results without signing in. Beautiful exploration, with a chain of evidence you can check.
