Astronomy archives contain extraordinary images, but exploring the evidence behind them usually requires specialist tools. Latent Sky makes that process approachable: choose a real sky field, reconstruct its image, inspect measured sources, and trace every result back to the original data. Our local prototype builds a galaxy mosaic and detects twenty sources near the Coma cluster, with nineteen catalog matches. The architecture pairs Supabase job tracking with separately attributed quality evidence. Our promise is simple: beautiful exploration, with a chain of evidence you can check.
